"Right now in American society, one of them most heated debate is on affirmative action. How much we should give the underrepresented minorities or disadvantaged people some preference or some preferential treatment. This has many different angles to look at the problem and it's a very complex problem. There are many different views but I am a very much in support of Affirmative Action. If we can all go into a totally colorblind society, that would be perfect but unfortunately we have not yet reached a stage. Even as an Asian-American, many people don't realize. In my case, they say 'you are chancellor of a great university.' I can tell you almost every day I have been somehow mistreated because of my color and my ethnicity. This is hard to understand for many people. That's why based on my personal experience we have not yet reached such a colorblind society." Exerpt from Sept 1996 interview with Dr. Chang-Lin Tien, the first Chinese Chancellor of UC Berkeley and US's first Asian to lead a major research university.
